What changed
------------
1. Long ranges no longer draw a daily chart. Past about a month the
   columns were thinner than their own labels and ran off the panel.
   Over 31 days it now shows submissions by week instead, stacked the
   same way, with a line saying why.

2. Between two and four weeks the daily chart thins its own labels
   rather than shrinking the bars: weekday names drop, dates angle,
   and only every second or third one prints.

3. "Latest week" now means the last COMPLETE Monday-to-Sunday week.
   An export pulled on a Monday carries one stray day of the new week,
   and the tab was opening on that -- so it showed 0 leads and 2 spam
   and looked broken. It now opens on Aug 10-16, matching the report.
   Last 4 and Last 8 weeks anchor the same way.

4. An in-progress week gets a dotted cap and is left out of the weekly
   average. Before, it dragged the average down and read as a collapse
   in volume.

5. The weekly average now also ignores the quiet weeks before the
   first lead ever arrived. It reads 25.6 leads a week, in line with
   the running average in the PDF. Previously 19.2, which was wrong in
   a way that would have understated the channel to Gregory.

6. A single-day range no longer stretches one bar across the panel.

Nothing about classification changed. Aug 10-16 still reads 29 leads,
21 spam, 0 tests, 58% signal quality, 24 sell / 2 buy.
