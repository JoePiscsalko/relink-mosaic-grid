What changed
------------
The tab now opens with a built-in sample: the classified output of the
August 17 export, covering April 30 through August 17 — sixteen weeks,
552 submissions.

Every identifying field was stripped before it went into the file. No
names, no emails, no company names, no message text. What remains is
the shape: when each submission arrived, what it asked for, where it
came from, and whether it was genuine. That is all the charts need,
and it is safe in a public repo.

Import a fresh export any time and it replaces the sample entirely.
