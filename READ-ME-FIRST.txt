CAMPAIGN BUILDER — small passes, so nothing times out
======================================================

Two files:

    src/CampaignBuilder.jsx
    netlify/functions/campaign.js

Add file -> Upload files, drag in BOTH folders, commit.
Includes everything from the last two updates.


WHY STAGE 1 CAME BACK EMPTY
----------------------------
The 60 second kill again, in a form I did not anticipate.

The market read is a five-part ask, so it generates close to its full
2,800 token allowance. At normal speed that is around 55 seconds — on
the wall. The function was killed mid-stream, the browser had received
nothing but keep-alive bytes, and the empty-stage guard correctly
reported an empty section.

The old stage 1 took 32 seconds because its ask was shorter and it
never used its full budget. Making the ask richer is exactly what
pushed it over.


THE FIX
-------
Every pass is now capped at 1,500 tokens rather than 2,000 to 3,600.

That works because a section can already ask for the rest of itself.
A small budget costs an extra round trip; a large one costs the whole
plan. So the budget is now sized for the slow case:

    at 60 tokens/sec   25s per pass, 35s spare
    at 50 tokens/sec   30s per pass, 30s spare
    at 35 tokens/sec   43s per pass, 17s spare

Continuations raised from 2 to 4 per section, since shorter passes
mean more of them are normal rather than a symptom.

An empty pass now retries once automatically before failing the run.
That case is almost always a request that died rather than the model
declining, and it is worth one more try before losing everything.


WHAT TO EXPECT
--------------
Three to four minutes, longer than before. More round trips, each one
small enough that none of them can fall off the wall. The stage
counter moves through the seven passes and text keeps flowing; you
will not see the continuations happen.

Wait for the counter beside THE PLAN to disappear before downloading
the pack — that is how you know all seven passes are finished.


THE TRADE I MADE
----------------
Slower and more reliable, over faster and occasionally losing the
whole run. Four failed attempts on this feature have all been the same
root cause in different clothes: I kept sizing for the good case. This
one is sized for the bad case.
