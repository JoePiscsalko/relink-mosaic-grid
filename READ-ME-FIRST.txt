WHAT YOU SAW
------------
"This section ran out of room and stopped early."

That message was working as designed — before it, a section would just
stop mid-row and say nothing. But telling you to generate the whole
plan again over one unfinished table is a poor answer.

The underlying problem is that I was guessing a token budget per
section. The keyword section asks for three grouped tables with four
columns, twelve to fifteen rows, negatives and a closing
recommendation. How long that runs depends entirely on the brief, so
no number I pick holds for every case.


THE FIX
-------
A section that runs out of room now asks for the rest of itself.

When the model hits the ceiling, the function sends an invisible
marker. The browser sees it and immediately requests a continuation,
handing back everything written so far with one instruction: pick up
exactly where you stopped, mid-sentence if that is where it broke,
do not repeat, do not restart, do not add a heading.

Up to two continuations per section. If it somehow still is not done
after that, you get a visible note rather than a silent stop.

You will not notice it happening. The text simply keeps going.

The keyword budget also went from 3,200 to 3,600 and the ask from
12-18 keywords to 12-15, so most runs will not need a continuation at
all.


ALSO WORTH KNOWING
------------------
In your screenshot the pack ended at Keywords, with the footer right
after. If the run was still going when you downloaded, the pack only
contains what existed at that moment. Wait for the stage counter to
disappear before downloading — that is how you know all seven passes
are done.
