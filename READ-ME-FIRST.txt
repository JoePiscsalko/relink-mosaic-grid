BENCHMARKS IN THE CELL DRAWER
==============================

This one needs two small edits to App.jsx as well as the new file.
The new file does all the work; the edits just switch it on.

STEP 1 — upload the new file
-----------------------------
Add file -> Upload files, drag in the "src" folder, commit.
Adds src/Benchmarks.jsx. Nothing is replaced.

STEP 2 — two edits to src/App.jsx
----------------------------------
Open src/App.jsx, click the pencil icon.

Edit A — the import.
Line 1 currently reads:

    import React, { useState, useMemo, useEffect, useRef } from "react";

Put this on the line directly below it:

    import BenchmarkPanel from "./Benchmarks.jsx";

Edit B — where the panel appears.
Use Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on Mac) inside the editor and search for:

    Leads by week

You want the line that reads, in full:

          {cell.trend && (<><h3 className="mg-dr-h3">Leads by week</h3><TrendChart data={cell.trend} color={color} /></>)}

Add this on its own line DIRECTLY ABOVE that line:

          <BenchmarkPanel channel={ch} totals={t} />

Commit changes.

That is the whole integration. If Netlify reports a build error, it is
almost always Edit B landing in the wrong spot — the line must sit
inside the same block as the one you searched for, not above the
function or after the closing brace.

WHAT YOU GET
------------
Click any cell in the grid and, under the KPIs, a "How this compares"
panel shows each metric against the range the market sits in, with a
marker for where reLink actually is and a plain-English sentence
saying what the metric means.

Metrics by channel:

  Google Ads     click-through rate, cost per click, conversion rate,
                 cost per lead, return on ad spend
  Paid Social    click-through rate, cost per click, CPM, cost per lead
  Email          open rate, click rate, click-to-open rate,
                 unsubscribe rate
  Display        click-through rate, cost per click, cost per lead

Rows only appear when the cell can actually answer them, so no row of
dashes. Where the sample is small — under ten leads, under a thousand
impressions — the panel says so in bold rather than letting a verdict
rest on four clicks.

The panel also prints, so the PDF export off a cell carries it.

ON THE NUMBERS
--------------
These are B2B and medical device benchmarks, not patient-facing
healthcare ones. Most published "healthcare marketing" data describes
clinics advertising to patients — a different auction with different
economics. reLink sells to health systems and equipment vendors, so
those figures would set the wrong bar in both directions.

Every metric carries its source, and the sources print in the panel
footnote so nobody has to take them on faith.

To change a number, edit the BENCHMARKS object at the top of
Benchmarks.jsx. Each entry has "weak" and "good" thresholds; the three
coloured bands are drawn from those two numbers.
