WHAT CHANGED — FormFills.jsx
-----------------------------
1. Ranges over 31 days show submissions by week, not by day. The daily
   chart was drawing 110 columns into a fixed panel and running off
   the edge.
2. Two to four week ranges thin their own labels instead of shrinking
   the bars.
3. "Latest week" now means the last COMPLETE Monday-to-Sunday week. An
   export pulled on a Monday carries one stray day of the new week and
   the tab was opening on it, showing 0 leads and looking broken.
4. An in-progress week gets a dotted cap and is excluded from the
   weekly average.
5. The weekly average also ignores the quiet weeks before the first
   lead arrived. It now reads 25.6 leads a week, in line with the
   running average in the PDF. It previously read 19.2, which
   understated the channel.
6. A single-day range no longer stretches one bar across the panel.

Classification is untouched. Aug 10-16 still reads 29 leads, 21 spam,
0 tests, 58% signal quality, 24 sell / 2 buy.


WHAT CHANGED — Benchmarks.jsx
------------------------------
Click any grid cell and a "How this compares" panel shows each metric
against the range the market sits in, with a marker for where reLink
is and a plain-English line on what the metric means.

  Google Ads     CTR, cost per click, conversion rate, cost per lead,
                 return on ad spend
  Paid Social    CTR, cost per click, CPM, cost per lead
  Email          open rate, click rate, click-to-open, unsubscribe
  Display        CTR, cost per click, cost per lead

Rows only appear when the cell can answer them. Small samples get
flagged in bold rather than carrying a verdict.

These are B2B and medical device benchmarks, not patient-facing
healthcare ones -- reLink sells to health systems and equipment
vendors, so clinic-to-patient figures would set the wrong bar. Every
metric prints its source in the panel footnote.

To change a number, edit the BENCHMARKS object at the top of the file.
Each entry has "weak" and "good" thresholds and the three coloured
bands are drawn from those two numbers.
