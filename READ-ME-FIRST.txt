NEW — click any entry for the full record
------------------------------------------
Rows in the Entries table are now clickable. A drawer slides in with:

  - who it was, the company, and the time it landed
  - the path and category they picked
  - the channel, and WHY it was attributed that way, in words
    ("Decided on: referred by google.com", "utm_source=relinkonline")
  - the landing page URL
  - the email as a clickable mailto link
  - the full message they wrote
  - for spam, the reason it was filtered

Newer / Older buttons walk the list without closing, and the arrow
keys do the same. Escape closes.

Important: the built-in sample has no contact details in it, so the
drawer will say "Contact details withheld" until you import the real
WPForms export in this tab. That is the privacy design working, not a
bug. The drawer says so on screen.


NEW — filtering the entries
----------------------------
Under the Leads / Spam / Tests pills:

  - a search box covering name, email, company, message, path,
    category, channel and spam reason
  - a channel dropdown
  - a path dropdown
  - a count showing how many of the total match

The dropdowns only offer values that exist in the selected date range,
so a filter never returns an empty list by surprise. The Newer/Older
buttons stay inside whatever you have filtered to, so you can filter to
Buy equipment and walk just those.


FORM FILLS — chart cleanup
---------------------------
1. Ranges over 31 days show submissions by week, not by day. The daily
   chart was drawing 110 columns into a fixed panel.
2. Two to four week ranges thin their own labels instead of shrinking
   the bars.
3. "Latest week" means the last COMPLETE Monday-to-Sunday week. An
   export pulled on a Monday carries one stray day of the new week and
   the tab was opening on it, showing 0 leads and looking broken.
4. An in-progress week gets a dotted cap and is excluded from the
   weekly average.
5. The weekly average ignores the quiet weeks before the first lead
   arrived. It now reads 25.6 leads a week, in line with the running
   average in the PDF. It previously read 19.2.
6. A single-day range no longer stretches one bar across the panel.

Classification is untouched. Aug 10-16 still reads 29 leads, 21 spam,
0 tests, 58% signal quality, 24 sell / 2 buy.


BENCHMARKS IN THE GRID
-----------------------
Click any grid cell and a "How this compares" panel shows each metric
against the range the market sits in, with a marker for where reLink
is and a plain-English line on what the metric means.

  Google Ads     CTR, cost per click, conversion rate, cost per lead,
                 return on ad spend
  Paid Social    CTR, cost per click, CPM, cost per lead
  Email          open rate, click rate, click-to-open, unsubscribe
  Display        CTR, cost per click, cost per lead

Rows only appear when the cell can answer them. Small samples get
flagged in bold rather than carrying a verdict.

These are B2B and medical device benchmarks, not patient-facing
healthcare ones. Every metric prints its source in the footnote. To
change a number, edit the BENCHMARKS object at the top of the file.
